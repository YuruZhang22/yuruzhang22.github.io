My major is Computer Science and my research interests include Wireless Communication, Digital Twin, Machine Learning, and Edge Computing.

Currently, I hold the position of Graduate Research Assistant at the [Intelligent Network sysTem (INT) Laboratory](https://liuqiang12040913.github.io/projects/) under the guidance of [Dr. Qiang Liu](https://liuqiang12040913.github.io/).
